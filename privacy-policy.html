Privacy Policy

For rclone (personal use) · Last updated: October 28, 2026

RClone is a private, personal-use application operated by Nick Craig-Wood, used by me under the terms of his, associated with www.mjzpeanut.com. It is designed for use by a single authorized user, in this case: Me.

This policy describes what information the App accesses through Google APIs and any other services, how it is used, and how it is protected or deleted.

1. Information the App accesses

With the account holder's authorization through Google's OAuth consent process, the App may access only the following information needed for its features:

  • Google services and user data: Google Drive.
  • OAuth permissions (scopes): https://www.googleapis.com/auth/docs, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly.
  • Authentication information: OAuth access tokens, and refresh tokens if the App requests offline access, used solely to maintain authorized API access.

The App does not request access to unrelated Google account information.

2. How information is used

The App uses the authorized data only to provide its personal-use functionality: Copying and cloning from my personal Google Drive to local storage mediums.

Actions performed on the user's behalf include reading file metadata, downloading files off of Google Drive and create or modify files..

The App does not use Google user data for advertising, targeted profiling, resale, or training generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

3. Storage, retention and deletion

Google user data is not stored anywhere, as I am the only user with access to this service. OAuth tokens are not stored either.

Data and any logs are retained for documentation purposes, and deleted within 24 hours..

To revoke Google account access, the account holder can use Google Account's third-party access settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized API requests but may not automatically erase data previously stored by the App. To request or perform deletion of previously stored data, ask me.

4. Sharing and disclosure

The App does not sell Google user data, share it with data brokers, or provide it to advertising platforms. Data is exchanged with Google APIs as needed to perform authorized operations.

Other third parties, hosting providers, analytics providers, or subprocessors that receive Google user data are: None. The App does not otherwise transfer Google user data except where permitted by the Google API Services User Data Policy, such as with the account holder's consent for the App's functionality, for security, or to comply with applicable law.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access to Google user data is limited to the App's disclosed functionality. The App does not use this data for unrelated purposes.

6. Security

Access to the App is limited to the single authorized Google Account, owned by me. The App protects credentials and any stored data using HTTPS and encrypted backup drives.

7. Your choices and rights

The authorized user controls whether to grant or revoke Google API permissions. They may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information retained by the App by contacting me via discord at @mjzpeanut, subject to applicable law.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised, and any material changes in the use of Google user data will be communicated to the authorized user before the new use begins, with additional consent obtained when required.
